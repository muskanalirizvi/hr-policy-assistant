# Acme Corp — Remote Work Policy
Effective: January 1, 2026 | Owner: People Operations

## 1. Hybrid Model
- Acme Corp follows a hybrid model: employees work from the office 3 days a week (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday are core office days).
- Monday and Friday are flexible remote days.

## 2. Fully Remote Work
- Fully remote arrangements require written approval from the Head of Department and HR.
- Employees on probation are not eligible for fully remote work.

## 3. Working From Abroad
- Employees may work from another country for up to 30 days per calendar year.
- Requests must be submitted to HR at least 14 days in advance.

## 4. Equipment
- Acme Corp provides a laptop, monitor, and headset to all employees.
- Remote employees receive a one-time home office allowance of $300.
- Internet reimbursement: up to $40 per month for employees with approved remote arrangements.

## 5. Working Hours
- Core collaboration hours are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM (company local time), regardless of location.
