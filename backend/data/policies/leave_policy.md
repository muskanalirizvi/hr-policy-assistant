# Acme Corp — Leave Policy
Effective: January 1, 2026 | Owner: People Operations

## 1. Annual Leave
- Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Leave accrues monthly at 1.67 days per month.
- Employees on probation (first 3 months) may not take annual leave, but accrual begins from day one.
- Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next year; carried-over days expire on March 31.

## 2. Sick Leave
- Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year.
- Sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days requires a medical certificate.
- Unused sick leave does not carry over.

## 3. Casual Leave
- Employees receive 6 days of casual leave per year for personal matters.
- Casual leave cannot be combined with annual leave in the same request.

## 4. Parental Leave
- Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave.
- Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave.
- Employees must have completed 6 months of service to be eligible.

## 5. How to Apply
- Leave requests must be submitted at least 7 days in advance (except sick leave).
- Requests are reviewed by the HR team and the employee's line manager.
- Requests longer than 10 consecutive working days require Head of Department approval.
