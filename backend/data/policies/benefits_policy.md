# Acme Corp — Employee Benefits
Effective: January 1, 2026 | Owner: People Operations

## 1. Health Insurance
- All full-time employees and their spouse and up to 2 children are covered under the company health plan.
- Annual coverage limit: $10,000 per family.
- Coverage begins on the first day after probation ends.

## 2. Learning & Development
- Each employee receives an annual learning budget of $1,000 for courses, certifications, and conferences.
- Unused learning budget does not carry over.
- Certification costs over $500 require manager approval.

## 3. Fitness Allowance
- Employees can claim up to $50 per month for gym memberships or fitness classes.
- Claims must be submitted with receipts by the 5th of the following month.

## 4. Bonuses
- Performance bonuses are paid annually in March, based on the yearly review.
- Employees who joined after October 1 of the review year are eligible for a prorated bonus.

## 5. Referral Bonus
- Employees who refer a candidate that is hired receive a $500 bonus after the new hire completes probation.
