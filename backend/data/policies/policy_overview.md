# Acme Corp — Policy Overview
Effective: January 1, 2026 | Owner: People Operations

## Company Policies at Acme Corp
Acme Corp has three main HR policies that every employee should know:
- Leave Policy: annual leave (20 days), sick leave (10 days), casual leave (6 days), parental leave, carry-over rules, and how to apply for leave.
- Remote Work Policy: the hybrid model (office on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday), fully remote arrangements, working from abroad (up to 30 days), equipment and allowances, and core working hours.
- Employee Benefits: health insurance, learning and development budget ($1,000), fitness allowance, performance bonuses, and referral bonuses.
For details on any policy, ask about that specific topic, for example "How does carry-over work?" or "What is the fitness allowance?".
